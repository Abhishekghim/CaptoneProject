Changes made: selected skeletal parts (skull, vertebral column, thoracic cage,
pelvic and shoulder girdles, limb bones) and organs (brain, spinal cord, liver,
kidneys, pancreas, urinary bladder) were merged into anatomical groups,
re-oriented to Y-up metres, mesh-simplified (about 200k triangles in total) and
meshopt-compressed. anatomy-poster.jpg is a render of that model.
